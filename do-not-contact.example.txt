# Do-not-contact list
#
# Copy to `do-not-contact.txt`. One entry per line: an email address, an email
# domain, or a profile URL. Blank lines and lines starting with # are ignored.
# Matching is case-insensitive.
#
# Anyone listed here is never contacted again, on any channel, in any run. The
# check runs before drafting, not just before sending — it is cheaper to skip
# someone than to explain a message you should not have sent.
#
# WHY THIS FILE LIVES OUTSIDE state/
# Clearing run state must not resurrect a person who asked you to stop. Keep
# this file, back it up, and mount it into your container. Losing it means
# re-contacting people who have already objected, which in several jurisdictions
# is a violation rather than an embarrassment.
#
# ADD, NEVER REMOVE. Suppress rather than delete the underlying record: a
# deleted contact gets re-added by the next CRM sweep, a suppressed one does not.
#
# Add someone the moment they ask — by reply, by unsubscribe, or in person.
# Most regimes give you a fixed window to honour the request, and the window is
# shorter than your next quarterly cleanup.
